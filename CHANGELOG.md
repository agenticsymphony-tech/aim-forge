## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added
- First-person 3D aim trainer running in the browser, with three.js vendored and no build step.
- Five guns, five animated knives and four operators.
- Five training modes, including Gridshot, Reflex Flick, Smooth Tracking and Bot Duel.
- Six themed maps.
- Settings with typed sensitivity, personal bests and score difference on results.
- Pause menu with R (restart) and Q (quit) shortcuts.
- Trailer, short README demo GIF, MIT license, contributing guide, issue forms and CI.
